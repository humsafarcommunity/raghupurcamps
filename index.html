Raghupur Fort Camps: stargazing tents on a Himalayan ridge

Raghupur Fort stands on an open ridge above Jalori Pass in Kullu district, Himachal Pradesh. We run tented camps on the ridge near the fort, with warm bedding, hot Himachali meals and a bonfire as the night sky comes out. It pairs easily with the Serolsar Lake trek, and Jibhi and Tirthan Valley are a short drive down the mountain.
