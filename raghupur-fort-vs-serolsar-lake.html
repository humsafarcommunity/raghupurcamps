Raghupur Fort vs Serolsar Lake

Two walks from Jalori Pass: a ridge climb to a fort, or a forest path to a sacred lake.

Call +91 70187 36861WhatsApp

Both walks start at Jalori Pass, and visitors often ask which is better. Here is how they compare, and how to fit both into one trip.

By Raghupur Fort Camps • Updated 22 Nov 2026

The short answer

If you have time for one walk from Jalori Pass, choose Serolsar Lake for a forest walk and a sacred lake, or Raghupur Fort for open ridge views and fewer people. With a full day you can do both, and with a night at the camp you can split them across two mornings.

Side by side

Raghupur FortSerolsar Lake
Distance from Jalori PassAbout 3 km one wayAbout 5 km one way

Best forSunrise, sunset, camping, photosFamilies, quiet walkers, culture
CrowdsUsually quieterBusier in season
Sacred siteSmall shrine on the ridgeBudhi Nagin temple on the lakeshore

Distances and times are approximate and differ between sources. Allow extra time for photos and rest at altitude.

Choose Raghupur Fort if

  • You want wide views over the ranges around Jalori Pass.
  • You are happy with a steeper climb but a shorter distance.
  • You plan to camp on the ridge or watch the sunset.

Read the Raghupur Fort guide.

Choose Serolsar Lake if

  • You prefer a gentle forest walk.
  • You are travelling with children or older family members.
  • You want the Budhi Nagin legend and temple.

Read the Serolsar Lake trek guide, and respect the lake: do not swim in it or walk on it when frozen.

A simple two-day plan

  • Day 1: reach Jalori Pass by late morning, walk to Serolsar Lake and back, then continue to camp.
  • Night: dinner, campfire and stargazing on the ridge.
  • Day 2: sunrise at Raghupur Fort, breakfast, then head down.

See the camping options and the Serolsar + camp itineraries.

When can you go?

Both trails depend on the Jalori Pass road, which is usually open from about March or April to early December and closed in peak winter. Check the road status guide before you plan.

Frequently asked questions

Is Raghupur Fort or Serolsar Lake better?

Raghupur Fort suits sunrise, sunset and views. Serolsar Lake suits an easy forest walk and the temple. Many visitors do both.

Which is harder, Raghupur Fort or Serolsar Lake?

Raghupur Fort is shorter but steeper. Serolsar Lake is longer but gentler.

Can I do both in one day?

It is possible for fit walkers who start early, but staying a night at the camp makes it more relaxed.

Is Raghupur Fort good for beginners?

Yes, with a slow pace and rests. It is a moderate climb above 3,000 m.

Is there an entry fee for either?

No, neither charges an entry fee.

Where can I stay near both?

Our Raghupur Fort camp is on the ridge above Jalori Pass, close to both trailheads.
