Jalori Pass ridge camping or Jibhi riverside camping?

Two different kinds of camp night in the same region.

Call +91 70182 36861WhatsApp

Camps around Jibhi and Tirthan Valley come in two broad styles: riverside camps low in the valley and high-altitude camps on ridges near Jalori Pass. They feel very different, so the right choice depends on the season, the sky you want and how cold you are willing to be.

By Raghupur Fort Camps • Updated 20 Nov 2026

Riverside camping in Jibhi and Tirthan

Riverside camps sit at lower altitude beside a stream or river. You fall asleep to the sound of water, the nights are milder and the setting is forest and village. Many travellers pair them with waterfall walks and a visit to Jibhi's sights. The trade-off is less open sky, so wide mountain views and stargazing are more limited.

Ridge camping near Jalori Pass

Our camp is on the Raghupur Fort ridge, in open meadow high above the valley. The view is long and the sky is wide, and with no town lights nearby the stars are the main event. Nights are cold even in summer, and you are above 3,000 m, so it is a more adventurous stay. Details are on our camping page.

Which one should you choose?

  • Choose the ridge if you want big views, stars and a trek in the same trip.
  • Choose the riverside if you want a gentler, warmer stay in the valley.
  • Do both if you have three days: valley first to acclimatise, then up to the ridge.

Best season for each

The ridge is best from April to November, with the clearest skies from September. Riverside camps are comfortable across a wider part of the year, but monsoon rain affects both. Check our road and season guide before you book.

Frequently asked questions

Is it colder at Jalori Pass than in Jibhi?

Yes. Jalori Pass and the ridge are above 3,000 m, so nights are noticeably colder than in the valley. Carry proper warm layers.

Can I do both in one trip?

Yes. A common plan is a day or night in Jibhi or Tirthan, then a day trek and a camp night on the Raghupur Fort ridge.

Is ridge camping safe for beginners?

Yes, with preparation. Walk slowly, drink water, dress warmly and tell our team if you feel unwell.

Which is better for stargazing?

The ridge, because it is high, open and far from town lights.
