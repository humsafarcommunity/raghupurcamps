Travel guide • Shoja

Shoja, Himachal: places to visit and how to reach

A quiet forest village below Jalori Pass, close to Serolsar Lake and Raghupur Fort.

Call +91 70187 36861WhatsApp

Shoja is a small village in the Seraj Valley, about 5 km below Jalori Pass. This guide covers what to see, how to reach it and when to go, plus how it compares with staying in Jibhi.

By Raghupur Fort Camps • Updated 22 Nov 2026

Where is Shoja?

Shoja (also spelt Shojha or Sojha) is a small village in the Seraj Valley of Kullu district, Himachal Pradesh, at roughly 2,368 m. It sits only about 5 km below Jalori Pass, among deodar and pine forest, with views of snow peaks on clear days. That makes it a calmer alternative to Jibhi, and a better base if you want an early start on the pass trails.

Things to do in and around Shoja

  • Waterfall Point. A short walk of about 1 km from the village to a small cascade in the forest.
  • Jalori Pass. Drive up for the Jalori Mata temple, open meadows and wide views. Reach the top by late morning for the clearest air.
  • Serolsar Lake. A sacred forest lake with a small temple to Budhi Nagin. See the Serolsar Lake trek guide.
  • Raghupur Fort. A ridge walk to the ruins above the pass with long views. See the Raghupur Fort guide.
  • Birdwatching. Himalayan monal, Indian blue robin and white-throated tit are reported in the forests nearby.
  • Tirthan Valley. A day trip towards Gushaini and the Great Himalayan National Park. See Jibhi and Tirthan Valley.

Shoja or Jibhi: where should you stay?

Jibhi has more cafes, homestays and short waterfall walks. Shoja is quieter and closer to the pass, so you save the early-morning drive when you want sunrise on the ridge. Many travellers split the stay: a night or two in Jibhi for the valley, then one night near the pass. If you want to sleep at altitude, our Raghupur Fort camp is on the ridge itself.

How to reach Shoja

  • From Delhi: take an overnight bus towards Manali, get off at Aut, then take a taxi through Banjar. Delhi to Jibhi is roughly 500 km and 10 to 13 hours.
  • From Chandigarh: about 250 km via Bilaspur, Mandi and Aut, around 7 to 8 hours.
  • From Shimla side: over Jalori Pass via Narkanda and Anni, only when the pass is open.
  • By air: Bhuntar (Kullu) is the nearest airport, around 2 to 2.5 hours by road.

Seasons and the Jalori Pass road

The road over Jalori Pass is seasonal. It usually reopens in March or April once the snow is cleared and closes in December. In January and February assume it is shut, and check before you travel. In monsoon, landslides can slow the road. Read the Jalori Pass road status guide before you plan.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Shoja from Jalori Pass?

Shoja is about 5 km from Jalori Pass by road.

What is the altitude of Shoja?

Shoja is at roughly 2,368 m above sea level.

What are the best places to visit near Shoja?

Waterfall Point, Jalori Pass, Serolsar Lake, Raghupur Fort and Tirthan Valley are the most popular.

Is Shoja better than Jibhi?

Shoja is quieter and closer to the pass, while Jibhi has more cafes and waterfall walks. Many travellers stay in both.

How many days do I need in Shoja?

Two to three days lets you see Waterfall Point, the pass and one trek.

Is the Jalori Pass road open in winter?

Usually not. It is generally closed from about January to March and reopens when the snow is cleared.

Can I camp near Shoja?

Yes. Our Raghupur Fort camp is on the ridge above Jalori Pass, a short trip from Shoja.
