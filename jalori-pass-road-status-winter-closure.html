Jalori Pass road: winter closure and monsoon tips

How to plan around snow and rain before you drive up.

Call +91 70187 36861WhatsApp

The road to Jalori Pass is a steep mountain road that is affected by weather. Snow can close it in winter, and heavy rain can cause landslides in the monsoon. This page explains the seasons and how to check conditions before you travel. It is general guidance, not a live road report.

By Raghupur Fort Camps • Updated 22 Nov 2026

Quick answer: Jalori Pass is usually open from about March or April to December and closed in peak winter, roughly January and February. Exact dates depend on snow clearance, so call us or check with local taxi drivers before you travel.

Winter: snow and closures

From late December to early March, snowfall can block the Jalori road, and the pass is sometimes reachable only on foot. Conditions change from day to day. If you plan a winter trip, expect it to be a snow trek, carry proper winter gear and keep your dates flexible.

Monsoon: July and August

Heavy rain, cloud and landslides can slow or block the road. Visibility drops and trails get slippery. If you travel in the monsoon, add buffer days, avoid driving at night and check conditions the day before.

How to check current conditions

  • Call us on +91 70187 36861 before you set out. We can tell you what we are seeing locally.
  • Ask taxi drivers in Jibhi or Banjar the same morning.
  • Check weather forecasts for Kullu and Shimla districts.
  • Keep a plan B, such as a stay in Jibhi, if the pass is closed.

Safe travel tips

  • Drive in daylight and avoid the pass in bad weather.
  • Keep fuel above half a tank, as pumps are far from the pass.
  • Do not rely on mobile signal, and share your plan with someone.
  • Carry warm clothes in the car even in summer.

Best months for a smooth trip

April to June and September to November are generally the easiest. See the season table and then book a camp night.

Frequently asked questions

Is Jalori Pass open in winter?

Snow can block the road from late December to early March. Call us before you travel for current conditions.

Is Jalori Pass safe in the monsoon?

Heavy rain can cause landslides on the road. Add buffer days, avoid night driving and check conditions first.

How do I check the Jalori Pass road status?

Call us on +91 70187 36861, ask local drivers in Jibhi or Banjar and check the weather forecast for the area.

Is there a fixed closing date every year?

No. It depends on snowfall, so the dates change from year to year.
