Serolsar Lake: a quiet forest lake near Jalori Pass

Serolsar Lake (also written Seroalsar or Serolsar Tal) is a small, calm lake set deep in forest just below Jalori Pass. The water mirrors the sky, shifting between green, blue and grey. A small temple to Budhi Nagin stands on the bank, and locals treat the lake as sacred. The trek starts at Jalori Pass, about 12 km above Jibhi, and you can end the day with a night at Raghupur Fort camp. Read the full Serolsar Lake trek guide.

The sacred legend

The legend of Mata Budhi Nagin

Local tradition holds that Serolsar is the home of Budhi Nagin, revered across the Seraj region as the mother of the Nag (serpent) deities. Villagers from Kullu and Mandi come to pay their respects, and offerings of ghee are made at her temple.

Visitors often remark on how clean the lake stays. A popular story credits a small bird, known locally as the Abhi, with picking fallen leaves from the water to keep the goddess’s lake clear.

Keep your shoes away from the water and your voice low. The quiet here is part of the place.

The trail journey

From Jalori Pass into the forest

A family-friendly walk that moves from open ridge into oak and rhododendron woods.

  1. 01

    Jalori Pass trailhead

    Start near the Kali Mata temple at the pass, with views across the Kullu and Shimla sides of the range.

  2. 02

    Oak and rhododendron woods

    A shaded trail carpeted with pine needles and lichen. In spring the rhododendrons bloom along the way.

  3. 03

    Forest clearings

    Open patches along the route make good rest stops, with views down into the forested gorges.

  4. 04

    Lake and temple

    The path drops into the forest basin where the lake lies still beside the Budhi Nagin temple and prayer flags.

Sacred lake etiquette

No swimming

The lake is holy water. Please do not step into it or wash in it.

Remove footwear at the shrine

Take your shoes off before approaching the temple and the path around it.

Leave no trace

Carry all plastic, bottles and wrappers back to Jalori Pass.
