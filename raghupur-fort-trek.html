Raghupur Fort trail and ancient ruins

A moderate climb of about 3 km from Jalori Pass, through rhododendron forest to the ruins of an old hill fort.

View the camp

Trek to the old fort above Jalori Pass

Raghupur Fort (also called Raghupur Garh) is believed to have been built by the Mandi rulers as a lookout and defence post. Only ruins remain today, but the setting is the draw: stone walls on an open ridge, with views over the surrounding valleys and, on clear days, snow-capped peaks. You can also camp overnight on the ridge or combine the walk with the Serolsar Lake trek. More in the Raghupur Fort history and what-to-see guide.

Heritage and trail

Walking the ridge of the old fort

The trail starts at Jalori Pass and quickly leaves the road crowd behind, climbing through oak and rhododendron woods before opening onto grassy alpine slopes.

Near the top the dry-stone ruins appear along the ridge. Walk the remains of the walls, look out over the valleys, and imagine the sentries who once watched this route.

On a clear afternoon you can pick out line after line of snow peaks, with birds of prey riding the air far below.

The trail

From Jalori Pass to the fort

Start early so you reach the top before afternoon cloud rolls in.

  1. 01

    Jalori Pass start

    Begin near the Kali Mata temple at the pass, with the trail climbing steadily into the trees.

  2. 02

    Oak and rhododendron forest

    A shaded, cool section with soft ground. Rhododendrons colour the trail in spring.

  3. 03

    Open meadow slopes

    The trees thin out and big alpine meadows appear, with wide views opening up behind you.

  4. 04

    The fort ruins

    Reach the old stone walls on the ridge, the highest point of the walk and the best view.

Trek tips

Start early

Mornings are usually clearest. Mountain weather can change quickly after midday.

Carry the basics

Water, a rain layer, sturdy shoes with grip, sun protection and a headlamp if you may finish late.

Take it slowly

You are above 3,000 m. Walk at an easy pace, drink water and turn back if you feel unwell.

Make it a night

Trek up, then stay on the ridge

Finish the climb in the afternoon and spend the night at camp for sunset, a bonfire and the stars.
